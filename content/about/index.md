I'm Lucas. I'm a Staff Machine Learning Engineer at Canva Research in Sydney. In my job I build agents and train LLMs to design using Canva. I'm half-way between a researcher and a product engineer, and not as competent as either. My writing here though is for my side projects and musings.

Outside of that, I'm a dad, a squash player, an aspiring cinephile and a general tinkerer.

All content on this blog is written by me. Artisanal, hand-placed tokens. I use LLMs to refine ideas but never to write. Images are generated with Midjourney.
